About

Strategy and working code.

I'm a French computer scientist, academic leader, and educational technology builder based in London. I design and build digital solutions that improve how universities support students and staff — combining pedagogy, technology, and strategy to create tools that are used in real environments.

My work focuses on practical problems in higher education: student-facing tools like degree calculators and learning support applications, data reporting systems, automation workflows, and AI-enhanced solutions. I work across the full stack, from identifying problems and designing solutions to building and deploying them.

Alongside my technical work, I've held senior academic leadership roles including Director for Learning and Teaching and Assistant Head of School in Computer Science and Engineering, leading initiatives in AI integration, assessment design, and student engagement.

My background includes contributing to the jQuery ecosystem as a jQuery Mobile team member, Documentation Lead, and elected board member of the jQuery Foundation. I'm also a regular speaker at international EdTech conferences, where I share practical approaches to using technology and AI in education.

I'm currently focused on my role at the University of Westminster, but I'm open to selective collaborations on EdTech projects — from scoping and technical design through to build and deployment.

My philosophy is simple: combine learning and teaching expertise with coding and strategic thinking to create meaningful, scalable impact in education.

Code Visualiser

Interactive Web App

An award-winning educational tool that converts code into animated SVG flowcharts in real-time, making programming concepts visible and concrete. Coding Escape Room

Pedagogical Design

A gamified learning and assessment system for a first-year Web Development module. Students escape by building real, working webpages — applying lecture content immediately through scaffolded challenges with automated, instant feedback. Piloted with 5 students, then deployed at scale to over 400. The same escape room format spans weekly formative tutorials and the summative in-class practical, lowering anxiety and giving students metacognitive control over their own assessment.
